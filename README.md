# Tata Data Visualisation

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Tata Data Visualisation Virtual Experience, focusing on analysing online retail transaction data and converting the analysis into meaningful business insights.

The objective was to explore revenue trends, country-level performance, customer revenue contribution, and geographic demand using Microsoft Power BI.

The analysis was designed to support business decision-making by identifying sales patterns, high-performing markets, valuable customers, and potential areas for growth.

---

## Business Objective

The business required an analysis of online retail transaction data to help senior stakeholders understand:

- How revenue changes over time
- Which countries generate the highest revenue and sales volume
- Which customers contribute the most revenue
- Which geographic markets show strong product demand
- Where potential opportunities for business expansion may exist

The analysis focused on producing clear and actionable insights rather than only presenting visualisations.

---

## Business Questions

### Q1. How does revenue trend over time?

The analysis examines monthly revenue patterns and identifies periods of higher and lower sales activity throughout the year.

### Q2. Which are the top 10 countries by revenue and quantity sold, excluding the United Kingdom?

The analysis compares countries based on revenue generated and quantity purchased to identify important international markets.

### Q3. Who are the top 10 customers by revenue?

The analysis identifies the customers contributing the highest revenue and examines the distribution of revenue across these customers.

### Q4. Which countries show strong product demand and potential expansion opportunities?

A geographic visualisation was used to understand product demand across countries, excluding the United Kingdom to focus on international opportunities.

---

## Dataset

The project uses an Online Retail transaction dataset containing information related to customer purchases.

Key fields used in the analysis include:

- Invoice Date
- Country
- Customer ID
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Revenue

---

## Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared before visualisation to improve the quality and relevance of the analysis.

The main preparation steps included:

- Removed transactions containing negative quantities.
- Removed transactions containing negative unit prices.
- Focused the analysis on valid sales transactions.
- Used the cleaned data for revenue, quantity, customer, country, and geographic analysis.

# Dashboard Analysis

## 1. Monthly Revenue Trend

### Objective

Analyse monthly revenue performance and identify periods of higher and lower sales activity.

### Visualisation

A line chart was used to display monthly revenue throughout 2011.

### Key Insight

Revenue fluctuated throughout the year, with a notable peak in May and another strong increase in November.

Revenue was comparatively lower in February and August.

### Business Implication

The business can use historical monthly patterns to support:

- Inventory planning
- Marketing campaign timing
- Sales forecasting
- Promotional planning
- Resource allocation during higher-demand periods

---

## 2. Top 10 Countries by Revenue and Quantity

### Objective

Identify the strongest international markets based on revenue and quantity sold, excluding the United Kingdom.

### Visualisation

A clustered column chart compares revenue and quantity across the top 10 countries.

### Key Insight

The Netherlands and Ireland show particularly strong performance compared with several other countries in both revenue and quantity sold.

Germany and France also represent significant markets, followed by Australia and other countries included in the analysis.

### Business Implication

These markets can be monitored closely for:

- Customer retention
- Targeted marketing
- Localised promotions
- Product availability
- Continued sales growth

---

## 3. Top 10 Customers by Revenue

### Objective

Identify the customers contributing the highest revenue.

### Visualisation

A column chart ranks the top 10 customers according to revenue generated.

### Key Insight

The analysis shows that revenue is distributed across several high-value customers, although the leading customer contributes substantially more revenue than the remaining customers.

The highest-revenue customer shown in the analysis is Customer ID 14646.

### Business Implication

High-value customers are important for maintaining recurring revenue. The business can consider:

- Customer retention strategies
- Personalised offers
- Loyalty initiatives
- Targeted communication
- Monitoring purchasing behaviour

At the same time, continuing to diversify the customer base can reduce dependency on a small number of high-value customers.

---

## 4. Product Demand by Country

### Objective

Understand geographic differences in product demand and identify markets that may require further investigation.

### Visualisation

A geographic map was used to display product demand based on quantity purchased, excluding the United Kingdom.

Larger bubbles represent countries with higher purchase quantities.

### Key Insight

The analysis shows strong demand across several European markets, particularly the Netherlands, Ireland, Germany, and France.

Other markets also show demand, although at comparatively lower levels.

### Business Implication

The business can investigate these markets further using:

- Customer research
- Market research
- Localised marketing
- Pricing analysis
- Product-demand analysis
- Distribution considerations

---

# Key Insights

### Revenue

- Monthly revenue varies throughout the year.
- May represents one of the strongest revenue periods.
- November also shows strong revenue performance.
- February and August show comparatively lower revenue.

### Countries

- The Netherlands and Ireland are among the strongest international markets.
- Germany and France also contribute significant revenue and quantity sold.
- Several European countries demonstrate strong demand.

### Customers

- Revenue is concentrated among a group of high-value customers.
- Customer ID 14646 is the highest-revenue customer shown in the analysis.
- Maintaining relationships with high-value customers can support recurring revenue.

### Geographic Demand

- European markets account for a significant portion of the international demand shown in the analysis.
- Markets with lower demand should be evaluated further before making expansion decisions.

# Business Recommendations

### 1. Plan Around Seasonal Revenue Patterns

Use historical revenue patterns to support inventory and marketing planning during stronger sales periods.

### 2. Strengthen High-Performing International Markets

Continue monitoring markets such as the Netherlands, Ireland, Germany, and France through targeted campaigns and customer-retention initiatives.

### 3. Focus on High-Value Customers

Develop retention strategies for customers generating significant revenue, including personalised communication and loyalty initiatives.

### 4. Diversify the Customer Base

While retaining high-value customers, continue developing new customer segments to reduce dependency on a limited number of customers.

### 5. Investigate Lower-Demand Markets

Before expanding into lower-performing regions, conduct additional research into:

- Product demand
- Pricing
- Customer preferences
- Competition
- Market potential

### 6. Use the Dashboard for Ongoing Monitoring

The Power BI dashboard can be used as a recurring management tool to monitor:

- Revenue trends
- Country performance
- Customer contribution
- Geographic demand
