# Tata Data Visualisation

Tata Data Visualisation Virtual Experience – Data Analysis and Business Insights Project

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the Tata Data Visualisation Virtual Experience. The objective was to analyse an online retail dataset and transform the data into clear, business-focused visual insights for senior stakeholders.

## Technologies and Approach

- Data: Online Retail transaction dataset
- Data Preparation: Removed records with negative quantities and negative unit prices.
- Visualisation: Time-series analysis, country ranking, customer ranking, and geographic/map visualisation.
- Business Analysis: Converted visual findings into practical recommendations for business decision-making.

## Key Business Questions

1. How does revenue trend over time, and are there significant seasonal patterns?
2. Which top countries generate the highest revenue and quantity sold?
3. Who are the top revenue-generating customers?
4. Which geographic markets show potential for future expansion?

## Key Insights

- Revenue shows noticeable seasonal variation, with stronger performance toward the end of the year.
- European markets are among the strongest revenue-generating regions.
- A relatively small group of customers contributes significantly to total revenue.
- Some geographic markets show lower demand and may require further research before expansion.

## Business Recommendations

- Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns ahead of the strong seasonal period.
- Continue strengthening high-performing European markets through targeted campaigns and retention activities.
- Maintain relationships with high-value customers while continuing to diversify the customer base.
- Investigate lower-performing geographic markets before expansion using localized pricing, product-demand analysis, and customer research.
- Use the dashboard and visual analysis as a recurring management tool to monitor revenue, customer value, and regional performance.

## Project Outcome

The project demonstrates the ability to transform transactional retail data into understandable visual insights and actionable business recommendations by combining data preparation, visual analysis, stakeholder-focused questions, and business conclusions.
